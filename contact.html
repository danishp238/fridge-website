Frequently Asked Questions

Do you charge for product reviews?

Never. Our reviews are completely independent. We earn through affiliate commissions when readers choose to buy through our links, but this never influences our ratings or recommendations.

Can I suggest a product for review?

Absolutely! Email us at hello@frostlogic.com with the product details. We review reader suggestions regularly and prioritize products with high demand.

How do affiliate links work?

When you click our affiliate links and make a purchase, we earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This is how we keep FrostLogic running and continue providing free, in-depth reviews.

Do you accept guest posts?

We occasionally feature guest articles from industry experts. If you have expertise in home appliances or electronics, reach out with your portfolio and topic ideas.
